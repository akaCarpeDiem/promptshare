Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026 · Applies to https://promptshare.fun/

What this site is

PromptShare is an educational curated gallery of AI image and video study plates, each shown with the prompt that created it. Browsing does not require an account. Public visitor publishing, uploads, and following feeds are not offered on the live site. Browsing does not send your words to an image, video, or audio model, and the site does not create a new output for you.

Infrastructure (honest stack)

PromptShare runs on Cloudflare Pages. Curated plate records live in Cloudflare D1. Media files (images now; video via R2 and later Cloudflare Stream) live in object storage — not inside the Pages deploy bundle — so the site shell stays small under the Pages size limit. Auth APIs may remain configured for operator use but are not offered as a public upload path. A local development server can keep the same records in a file on that machine. That file is not the production database.

AI-generated content

Plates on PromptShare show AI-generated media: still images, video, animation, audio, and small playable games. Each plate is shown with the prompt that belongs to that file. Publisher plates name the maker and link to the page that released the pair. Studio plates are labeled PromptShare studio when PromptShare wrote the prompt and generated the file. An output can be fictional or unlike a record of a real event. It is a study pair, not a statement of fact.

Information we collect

  • Account, if you sign in. Google, Microsoft, or an email magic link confirms an email address. We store that address, a display name, a unique handle, and the profile you choose to publish: bio, website, up to three social links, and a profile picture. We do not store your Google or Microsoft password, and we do not store a site password.
  • Session. A signed-in browser keeps an HttpOnly cookie named ps_session for 30 days. Magic-link tokens expire after 20 minutes. OAuth state tokens expire after 15 minutes.
  • Curated plates (operator content). Gallery media is published by the site operator into Cloudflare R2 (and Stream later for video). Plate metadata (prompt, model, tags, title) lives in D1. Public visitor uploads are disabled.
  • Optional image safety scan. When Sightengine API credentials are configured on the server, uploaded images (including profile pictures) may be sent to Sightengine to check for nudity/sexual content and minors-related signals before the file is stored. This is an automated, imperfect check — it is not 100% accurate and does not replace reports or human review. If those credentials are not configured, uploads are not sent to Sightengine; text-based prohibited-content checks still run.
  • Follows, likes, saves, and reports. These are stored against your account. A report includes the creation, your account, a short reason, and the time.
  • Technical / server logs. Hosting (Cloudflare Pages and related edge infrastructure) may automatically receive standard request metadata such as IP address, user agent, timestamps, and requested URLs for security, reliability, and abuse prevention.
  • Browser storage. The gallery stores the category you last chose in localStorage under the key ps-category. That value stays on your device. Details are on the Cookies page.
  • Contact. If you email or message us, we receive whatever you choose to send so we can respond.

Analytics

PromptShare does not currently run a first-party analytics product and does not set an analytics cookie. Cloudflare’s hosting edge may keep standard request logs as described above. If we add analytics later, it would be pageviews only. We would not send full prompts, plate images, or account emails to that analytics tool.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

The site loads the Google AdSense script with publisher ID ca-pub-4059575347806121 for Google’s site review. When ads are approved and shown, Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads, measure performance, and (where applicable) personalize ads based on your browsing activity across sites. You can learn more and manage ad settings at Google’s Advertising policies and Ads Settings.

Ad slots are not placed above a plate image. See also the Cookies page and ads.txt.

Third-party media and hosts

Gallery files are served with this site from Cloudflare. Each plate links to the publisher page that released the prompt and output (for example Google DeepMind, xAI, or Stability AI). Those sites, and Cloudflare, have their own privacy practices. PromptShare does not control their servers or policies. Google’s ad systems are an additional third party when ads are active, as described above.

How we use information

  • Operate the curated Images, Videos, and Games gallery
  • Operate and improve the gallery
  • Diagnose outages and abuse
  • Remember a category preference on your device
  • Respond to contact and deletion-request messages
  • Display advertising via Google and partners as described above

Deletion requests

To ask us to delete account data or an upload, email support@promptshare.fun. Use the address on the account when possible, and say what you want removed.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy as the gallery, analytics, or advertising setup changes. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.
