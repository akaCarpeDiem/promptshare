Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026 · Applies to https://promptshare.co/

What this site is

PromptShare is an educational curated gallery of AI images, videos, and small browser games, each shown with the prompt that created it. The site has no accounts and no sign-in. Public visitor publishing, uploads, profiles, follows, likes, and saves are not offered. Browsing does not send your words to an image, video, or audio model, and the site does not create a new output for you.

Infrastructure (honest stack)

PromptShare runs on Cloudflare Pages. Curated plate records live in Cloudflare D1. Media files (images now; video via R2 and later Cloudflare Stream) live in object storage — not inside the Pages deploy bundle — so the site shell stays small under the Pages size limit. The site has no sign-in code and sends no email. A local development server can keep the same records in a file on that machine. That file is not the production database.

AI-generated content

Plates on PromptShare show AI-generated media: still images, videos, and small playable games. Each plate is shown with the prompt that belongs to that file. Every plate in the gallery is a PromptShare studio plate: PromptShare wrote the prompt and generated the file with the model named on the plate. An output can be fictional or unlike a record of a real event. It is a study pair, not a statement of fact.

Information we collect

  • No accounts. The site does not offer sign-in, so browsing creates no account, profile, or session cookie. If you signed in during an earlier version of the site, we may still hold the email address, display name, handle, and profile details you gave then. Email us to have them deleted (see Deletion requests).
  • Game scores. Games keep a Top 10. If you play, we store the display name you choose and your scores. Your browser keeps a random player id and a secret token in localStorage so you can rename yourself later; our server keeps only a hash of that token. A game page also sets one first-party cookie, ps_voter, a random id that lets us recognise scores posted from the same browser. To limit abuse we store a salted hash of your IP address that changes every day; we do not store the address itself with your scores. Display names are checked by an automated filter before they are saved, and for that check the name you type is sent to a Cloudflare Workers AI model.
  • Curated plates (operator content). Gallery media is published by the site operator into Cloudflare R2 (and Stream later for video). Plate metadata (prompt, model, tags, title) lives in D1. Public visitor uploads are disabled.
  • No image uploads from visitors. PromptShare publishes its own plates. Visitors cannot upload files, so no visitor images are sent to any third-party scanning service.
  • Questions and removal requests. There are no accounts, sign-in, follows, likes, or saves. To ask about or flag a plate, contact support@promptshare.co. If you email us, we receive your message and email address so we can review the request and reply.
  • Technical / server logs. Hosting (Cloudflare Pages and related edge infrastructure) may automatically receive standard request metadata such as IP address, user agent, timestamps, and requested URLs for security, reliability, and abuse prevention.
  • Browser storage. The gallery keeps a few display preferences in your browser: light or dark mode if you use the switch, the card style you pick on a gallery page, and your video quality choice. These values stay on your device and are not sent to our servers. Details are on the Cookies page.
  • Contact. If you email or message us, we receive whatever you choose to send so we can respond.

Analytics (Google Analytics 4)

PromptShare uses Google Analytics 4, a service of Google LLC, to measure visits and how pages are used, such as which pages are viewed, roughly where visits come from, and what kind of device and browser are used. Google Analytics sets cookies (_ga and _ga_<ID>) and collects usage data like the page address, referring page, approximate location, and device information. We use this data only in aggregate to measure traffic and improve the site. We do not send email addresses or game display names to Google Analytics, and we do not use it to identify you personally. Cloudflare’s hosting edge may also keep standard request logs as described above.

Google processes this data under its own policies. See How Google uses information from sites that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. You can opt out by blocking or clearing cookies in your browser settings or by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

The site loads the Google AdSense script with publisher ID ca-pub-4059575347806121 for Google’s site review. When ads are approved and shown, Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads, measure performance, and (where applicable) personalize ads based on your browsing activity across sites. You can learn more and manage ad settings at Google’s Advertising policies and Ads Settings.

Ad slots are not placed above a plate image. See also the Cookies page and ads.txt.

Third-party media and hosts

Gallery files are served with this site from Cloudflare. A plate names the model that generated it (for example Grok Imagine or Midjourney); browsing a plate does not contact that model’s maker. Cloudflare, and any site we link to, has its own privacy practices. PromptShare does not control their servers or policies. Google’s ad systems are an additional third party when ads are active, as described above.

How we use information

  • Operate the curated Images, Videos, and Games gallery
  • Operate and improve the gallery
  • Diagnose outages and abuse
  • Remember display preferences on your device
  • Run game leaderboards and keep them free of abusive names
  • Respond to contact and deletion-request messages
  • Display advertising via Google and partners as described above

Deletion requests

To ask us to delete a leaderboard name or score, or account data from an earlier version of the site, email support@promptshare.co. Say what you want removed. For a leaderboard entry, give the game and the display name; for old account data, write from the address that was on the account when possible.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy as the gallery, analytics, or advertising setup changes. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.
