Cookies

Last updated: September 27, 2026 · Applies to https://promptshare.fun/

What this page covers

PromptShare is a creator network and an educational gallery. This page describes browser storage used by the site, including the session cookie set when you sign in, and the cookies Google AdSense may set when ads are active. Browsing does not require an account. Signing in does.

Storage the gallery uses now

The site saves one preference in localStorage, not in an HTTP cookie: the key ps-category holds the category chip you last chose (for example Animation or Street). That value stays in your browser. PromptShare’s servers do not receive it. Clearing site data for this host removes it. A first visit with nothing stored opens on Animation.

  • When you sign in, PromptShare sets one first-party cookie, ps_session. It is HttpOnly and SameSite=Lax, lasts 30 days, and identifies your session. It is not used for advertising. Signing out clears it.
  • PromptShare does not set an analytics cookie.
  • PromptShare does not set its own advertising cookie. Google may set cookies when AdSense runs, as described below.
  • Hosting (Cloudflare) may keep standard request logs, described in the Privacy Policy. Those logs are not a cookie you can read in the gallery.

Advertising cookies (Google AdSense)

PromptShare loads the Google AdSense script with publisher ID ca-pub-4059575347806121 for site review. When ads are approved and shown, Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads, measure how those ads perform, and, where allowed, personalize ads from visits across sites. Ad slots are not placed above a plate image. See also ads.txt.

You can read Google’s description and manage ad personalization here:

  • Google’s Advertising policies and technologies
  • Google Ads Settings
  • How Google uses cookies

How to control storage

  • Use your browser’s site settings to clear cookies and site data for promptshare.fun. That removes ps-category.
  • Private browsing usually drops localStorage when the session ends.
  • If AdSense is later enabled, use Ads Settings to control personalized ads, and your browser to block or delete third-party cookies.
